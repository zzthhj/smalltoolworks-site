Privacy policies

Each SmallToolWorks app has its own privacy policy, because each app handles different files, local records and product interactions. Each policy explains local processing, limited analytics and purchases. Read the policy for the app you actually use.

OmniPDF Privacy Policy Local PDF files, product analytics and purchases. SeeClear Privacy Policy On-device photo analysis and purchases. SizeFixer Privacy Policy Photo processing, redaction and metadata. NativeID Privacy Policy ID and passport photo preparation data.

This website

smalltoolworks.com is a static site. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or advertising scripts, loads no third-party JavaScript and embeds no trackers. There is no consent banner because there is no optional tracking script to control.

Fonts are served from your own system, and images are plain files on this same domain. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may process your IP address, browser information and request logs to deliver and protect the site. See the Cloudflare privacy policy.
