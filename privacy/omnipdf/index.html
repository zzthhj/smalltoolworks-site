1. Purpose

The app OmniPDF is designed to help you inspect, process, repair, organize, and export PDF files. Its features may include PDF issue detection, batch processing, merging, splitting, compression, rotation, page deletion or extraction, OCR text recognition, encryption and decryption, privacy cleanup, text redaction, document scanning, photo-to-PDF conversion, signatures, watermarks, and page numbering.

OmniPDF is a PDF processing tool. It does not provide legal, financial, identity verification, certification, or official document approval services.

2. Data Privacy

PDF checks, repair, compression, OCR, redaction, passwords, page editing and export run locally on your device. Document contents, OCR text, passwords and signatures are not uploaded to us. The app keeps local copies of recent documents and a recent-file list containing names, paths, page counts and file sizes. Clearing recent files removes managed copies; files exported outside the app remain where you saved them.

2.1 Anonymous Product Analytics

OmniPDF uses Aptabase to collect limited anonymous analytics so we can understand feature usage and improve the app. Analytics may include app launches, taps on major entry points, tool identifiers, completion or cancellation results, coarse file-count and duration ranges, export activity, paywall interactions, and anonymous purchase-plan and purchase-result events.

Aptabase may automatically attach a temporary session identifier, timestamp, language, operating-system name and version, app version and build number, SDK version, device model, and Debug/Release status. Aptabase may temporarily process the IP address and User-Agent required to receive the network request. OmniPDF does not send file names, paths, document contents, OCR text, passwords, signatures, free-form text, exact file sizes, exact page counts, Apple ID, advertising identifiers, or persistent device identifiers.

Using the IP address and User-Agent, Aptabase generates a coarse region and a pseudonymous identifier that changes every day. Analytics data is processed and stored in Aptabase’s European Union region and may be retained for up to five years. It is not linked to your identity, used for cross-app or cross-website tracking, advertising, profiling, or data-broker sharing, and is not sold. See the Aptabase Privacy Policy. OmniPDF contains no advertising and requires no account or login.

3. Files, Photos, and Camera

OmniPDF may request access only when needed to:

  • import PDF files, images, or documents from Files;
  • select photos from your library;
  • scan documents using your device camera;
  • save processed results to Files or Photos when you choose.

These permissions are used only for the functions you actively use inside the app.

4. PDF Processing

All PDF detection, repair, compression, OCR, batch processing, redaction, encryption, decryption, page editing, signatures, watermarks, and export operations are performed locally on your device.

If you use OCR, the recognized text is generated on your device and is not sent to us.

If you use password-related features, passwords are used locally only for opening, encrypting, or decrypting the selected PDF file. We do not receive or store your passwords.

5. Exported Files and Metadata

When you export a file, OmniPDF may create a new processed PDF according to your selected actions. Depending on the tools you use, OmniPDF may remove or modify hidden information such as document metadata, annotations, editable content, attachments, or other PDF information.

If you use text redaction or privacy cleanup, please review the output file before sharing it to make sure the result meets your needs.

6. In-App Purchases

Optional purchases, save credits, subscriptions, or paid exports are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment card, billing information, or Apple ID details.

Additional details

Purpose

OmniPDF processes PDFs in batches and combines operations into reusable workflows, with individual document tools and optional inspection.

Documents and local storage

PDF checks, repair, compression, OCR, redaction, passwords, page editing and export run locally on your device. Document contents, OCR text, passwords and signatures are not uploaded to us. The app keeps local copies of recent documents and a recent-file list containing names, paths, page counts and file sizes. Clearing recent files removes managed copies; files exported outside the app remain where you saved them.

Permissions

File, photo-library or camera access is used for the features you choose, such as importing, capturing or saving content. You can manage camera and photo permissions in iOS Settings. No account or sign-in is required.

Limited product analytics

We use Aptabase in its European Union region to understand feature usage and improve the app. Events are sent automatically when analytics is active; they are not a report you must submit manually.

App launches, entry and tool selections, completion or cancellation, coarse file-count and duration ranges, exports, paywall interactions, and purchase or restore outcomes.

The SDK attaches technical fields such as timestamps, temporary session identifiers, app and build versions, SDK and operating-system versions, device model, language and Debug/Release status. Aptabase processes the request IP address and User-Agent to derive a coarse region and a daily changing pseudonymous identifier. We do not send your name, email address, Apple ID, photos, advertising identifier or a persistent device identifier to Aptabase.

According to Aptabase's published policy, analytics may be retained for up to five years. Without a persistent personal identifier, it may not be possible to locate an individual person's events for deletion. See the Aptabase privacy policy.

Purchases and advertising

The current app does not display third-party advertisements. Optional purchases, credits or subscriptions are handled by Apple. The app reads transaction and entitlement information to unlock access; we do not receive your payment-card details or Apple ID. Limited plan categories and purchase outcomes may be recorded in product analytics.

Processing and result previews are available in the app. Exporting or sharing processed files requires an in-app purchase. Choose export credits or a monthly, yearly or lifetime plan; current options and prices are shown in the app and the App Store.

Your choices and retention

You can manage permissions, remove saved content where the app provides deletion controls, and uninstall the app to remove its local app data. Copies saved to Photos, Files or device backups are managed separately. Core processing is local; analytics, attribution and App Store purchases may use network connections. This app does not currently offer a dedicated analytics opt-out switch.

Support and policy changes

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send, and use them to respond. Send only the information needed to explain your issue. You may contact us about privacy questions or deletion of support correspondence.
