Privacy Policy

SeeClear · Privacy Policy

SeeClear 1.50 privacy: local processing, limited analytics, Apple Ads attribution and non-renewing 1-Day Pass purchases.

Updated 17 October 2026 · Version 1.50

Scope and local processing

SeeClear provides everyday color assistance, not medical diagnosis or safety-critical guidance. Photos, camera frames, imported document contents, recognition, enhancement and detailed analysis results are processed on your device and are not uploaded to us or Aptabase. No account is required.

Local storage

Saved photos, analysis records, colors, calibration preferences, free-use counters, purchase status and cached Apple Ads attribution may remain on your device. You can remove saved content using available app controls or uninstall the app to remove its local data. Files exported to Photos or Files and device backups are managed separately. StoreKit transaction history is managed by Apple.

Limited usage analytics

SeeClear automatically sends limited events to Aptabase in the European Union to understand blocked operations, improve convenience and features, and measure purchases and promotion. Events include app launches, feature choices, workflow steps, whether a result was produced, time spent in seconds, paywall interactions, plan categories, purchase and restore outcomes, and fixed technical failure codes such as denied permissions, processing errors or results not delivered. A scene without a target color is not itself a technical failure. Calibration events record only workflow steps; we do not send color-vision types, calibration answers or results, selected color groups, actual color values, photos, document contents or detailed analysis results.

Technical data and retention

The Aptabase SDK adds timestamps, temporary session IDs, app/build and SDK versions, operating-system information, device model, language and Debug/Release status. Requests also expose an IP address and User-Agent to Aptabase. Its published policy describes a pseudonymous identifier derived from IP/User-Agent with a salt changed every 24 hours, and analytics retention of up to five years. We do not send your name, email, Apple ID, IDFA or a persistent device identifier in these events. Individual events may not be identifiable for deletion. See the Aptabase privacy policy: https://aptabase.com/legal/privacy .

Apple Ads attribution

To measure App Store promotion, the app may send an attribution token to Apple through AdServices. The app caches limited attribution results and may include campaign ID, keyword ID, country/region, conversion category and attribution status in analytics and purchase events. The attribution token is not sent to Aptabase. This does not access the IDFA or track activity across other companies’ apps and websites.

Purchases and the 1-Day Pass

Apple handles in-app payments. SeeClear reads verified transactions and entitlements to provide Pro access; we do not receive your payment-card details or billing address. Version 1.50 adds a non-renewing 1-Day Pass: all Pro features for 24 continuous hours from the verified purchase time, without automatic renewal. Restoring uses the original purchase time and does not restart the 24 hours. Expired or revoked passes do not grant access. Prices and available plans are shown in the app and App Store. Limited plan categories and purchase outcomes may be sent to Aptabase; receipts and transaction IDs are not included in our analytics events.

Advertising

Version 1.50 does not display third-party ads and does not integrate the AdMob SDK. Apple Ads install attribution measures promotion of SeeClear in the App Store; it is separate from displaying ads inside SeeClear. Any future ad integration will require an updated policy before release.

Permissions and your choices

Camera, photo-library and file access are used for the tasks you choose. Manage camera and photo permissions in iOS Settings. Core processing runs locally; analytics, attribution and purchases use network connections. Version 1.50 has no dedicated in-app analytics opt-out switch. Restricting network access, where supported by your system, can also affect purchases and restore.

Support and policy updates

If you contact rtx3070757@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send to respond to your request. Do not send color-vision results, personal photos or account credentials unless needed for your request; never send passwords. You can contact us about privacy or deletion of support correspondence. We update this policy when practices change. Support: https://smalltoolworks.com/support/ .
