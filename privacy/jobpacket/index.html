JobPacket · Privacy Policy

Job records, photos, signatures and PDFs stay on your device. Limited usage analytics use Aptabase in the EU.

2026-10-25

Scope and local records

This policy covers JobPacket for iPhone and iPad, provided by SmallToolWorks. No account is required. Customer and site details, addresses, job notes, work performed, materials, issues, photos, voice-note audio, client signatures, the business profile and logo, and generated PDFs are stored in the app’s own storage on your device. They are not uploaded to us. Internal notes are kept out of client PDFs.

Permissions and your choices

The camera is used only when you take a job photo. The system photo picker provides only the photos you select. The microphone records voice notes, and speech recognition runs on the device. Location is optional: coordinates are added to a photo or a site only after you enable location or tap Use Current Location, and they appear in the PDF only when Include location in report is on. Saving a PDF or photo to Photos asks for add-only access; you can decline and share instead. Manage permissions in iOS Settings.

Local storage and retention

Job records, photos, audio, signatures, PDFs and export archives stay in the app container. Deleting a job moves it to Recently Deleted; after 30 days the job and its media are erased, and Delete Now erases them immediately. Deleting the app removes its local data. Device backups are managed by iOS.

Payments and restoration

Apple handles payments. The app verifies StoreKit transactions locally to unlock Pro; we do not receive card details or your Apple Account password. The first two finalized Job Packets are free. Pro is a monthly or yearly subscription, or a lifetime purchase, and can be restored with the purchasing Apple Account.

Usage analytics in the EU

JobPacket sends fixed event categories for app opens, job creation, start, outcome, finalization, revision and deletion, photos, notes, materials, issues, signatures, dictation and voice notes, report previews and exports, paywall views, and purchase and restore outcomes to Aptabase in the EU region. Properties contain only fixed categories and small count buckets (for example 1–3 or 4–9). Customer names, company names, addresses, photo content, captions, notes, signatures and any other text you enter are never sent. The SDK also sends timestamps, a temporary session identifier, language, app and build versions, operating-system version, device model and Debug/Release status. Aptabase processes IP addresses and User-Agent to derive coarse country or region and a pseudonymous identifier that changes daily for each app; its policy states retention of up to five years. There is no advertising SDK, no cross-app tracking and no tracking prompt. The app has no dedicated analytics opt-out switch; analytics stop if you delete the app.

Support and updates

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send, and use them to respond. Contact us for privacy questions or to request deletion of support correspondence. We update this policy when data practices change.
