JobPacket · Privacy Policy

JobPacket for iPhone and iPad is provided by SmallToolWorks and requires no account.

2026-11-11

Local records and scope

JobPacket for iPhone and iPad is provided by SmallToolWorks and requires no account. Customer and site details, quote requirements and site photos, agreed prices, work notes, materials, costs, issues, photos, voice recordings, signatures, business details and logo, invoices, payment and refund records, receipts and generated PDFs are stored on your device. These business records are not uploaded to our servers. Internal notes are excluded from customer PDFs.

Permissions

The camera is used when you take photos; the system photo picker provides only selected items. The microphone is used for voice recordings and dictation. Speech recognition requires supported on-device recognition. Location is optional and used only when requested or enabled; coordinates appear in reports only when you include them. Saving to Photos uses add-only permission. You can manage permissions in iOS Settings.

Sharing, backups and deletion

You choose which quotes, reports, invoices, receipts or other files to share and with whom. Site photos appear in quote PDFs only when you include them. Full backups and transfer archives can contain business records, media and business settings. JobPacket does not encrypt these archives; store and share them carefully. Files, iCloud Drive and other destinations you select are governed by their providers' policies. JobPacket does not upload your business database to our servers through these exports. Deleted jobs remain in Recently Deleted for up to 30 days unless permanently deleted sooner; cleanup occurs when the app runs. Removing the app removes its local data. iOS device backups and copies you exported are managed separately.

Purchases and business payments

Apple processes purchases of JobPacket Pro. The app verifies StoreKit transactions; we do not receive your card details or Apple Account password. Available plans include weekly, monthly and yearly subscriptions and lifetime access; availability, prices and trial eligibility are shown by the App Store. Customer payments, deposits, refunds and receipts entered in JobPacket are business records only: recording them does not transfer money or process a bank payment.

Optional usage diagnostics

Usage diagnostics are off by default. If you choose to share them, the app sends limited events to Aptabase in the EU to improve features and investigate failures: app opens, quote and work actions, photos and voice feature outcomes, PDF and receipt sharing, billing actions, backups and transfers, and purchase or restore outcomes. Properties contain fixed categories, count, duration and age ranges, plan identifiers, and random session, event and local workflow identifiers. System information includes app/build version, OS version, device model, language and timestamps. Diagnostics exclude customer names, contact details, addresses, real job or quote numbers, entered text, photos, audio, signatures and actual payment amounts. There are no advertising SDKs or cross-app advertising tracking.

Analytics processing and control

Aptabase temporarily processes the connection IP address and User-Agent to derive country/region and a daily per-app pseudonymous identifier. It states that IP addresses and User-Agent are not stored, salts rotate daily, and analytics are retained for up to five years. This approximate location is separate from optional GPS coordinates in your records. You can turn Share usage diagnostics off in Settings at any time. This clears pending events; it does not retract events already sent. Offline diagnostics are queued locally for up to 7 days and at most 2,000 events. Because analytics are not linked to your identity, individual events may not be identifiable for a deletion request.
