Complete privacy policy

1. Purpose: The App NativeID is strictly designed for creating ID photos. It does not perform any other functions.

2. Data Privacy: Photos and document processing take place on your device. The app may retain local content and preferences. Limited analytics, Apple install attribution where used, and purchases may use network connections. See the additional details below.

3. Anonymous Analytics: NativeID collects anonymous usage statistics (e.g. which tool is used, duration ranges, whether a purchase is completed) through the third-party service Aptabase . If you install the App after tapping an App Store ad, Apple’s official ad-attribution interface may return ad source information (such as keyword ID and country) to measure advertising effectiveness. We do not send photos or document contents in these analytics events; technical request data and temporary identifiers are described below.

4. Camera & Photo Library: Access is used exclusively to capture or select a photo for ID creation.

  • All processing is performed locally on your device.
  • Your photos are never uploaded to any server.

Additional details

Purpose

NativeID only prepares photos to specified size and layout requirements and does not create, issue, reproduce or modify official identity documents.

Photos and local history

Photo sizing, framing, background adjustment and print layouts run on your device. Photos are not uploaded to us for processing. Prepared photos, originals used for re-editing and history records may be stored locally. You can delete history inside the app. Exported photos remain where you saved them. Face positioning does not create an identity database or send biometric templates to us.

Permissions

File, photo-library or camera access is used for the features you choose, such as importing, capturing or saving content. You can manage camera and photo permissions in iOS Settings. No account or sign-in is required.

Limited product analytics

We use Aptabase in its European Union region to understand feature usage and improve the app. Events are sent automatically when analytics is active; they are not a report you must submit manually.

App launches, selected photo specifications, capture source, coarse duration ranges, export outcomes, paywall interactions, and purchase or restore outcomes. Photos, identity numbers and personal identity details are not sent.

The SDK attaches technical fields such as timestamps, temporary session identifiers, app and build versions, SDK and operating-system versions, device model, language and Debug/Release status. Aptabase processes the request IP address and User-Agent to derive a coarse region and a daily changing pseudonymous identifier. We do not send your name, email address, Apple ID, photos, advertising identifier or a persistent device identifier to Aptabase.

According to Aptabase's published policy, analytics may be retained for up to five years. Without a persistent personal identifier, it may not be possible to locate an individual person's events for deletion. See the Aptabase privacy policy.

Apple Ads attribution

The app uses Apple's AdServices interface to measure installs from App Store ads. It may exchange an attribution token with Apple and receive campaign, ad-group, keyword and country or region information. Limited source identifiers and attribution results are included in Aptabase events to measure campaign performance. This does not upload your photos or use an IDFA.

Purchases and advertising

The current app does not display third-party advertisements. Optional purchases, credits or subscriptions are handled by Apple. The app reads transaction and entitlement information to unlock access; we do not receive your payment-card details or Apple ID. Limited plan categories and purchase outcomes may be recorded in product analytics.

Your choices and retention

You can manage permissions, remove saved content where the app provides deletion controls, and uninstall the app to remove its local app data. Copies saved to Photos, Files or device backups are managed separately. Core processing is local; analytics, attribution and App Store purchases may use network connections. This app does not currently offer a dedicated analytics opt-out switch.

Support and policy changes

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send, and use them to respond. Send only the information needed to explain your issue. You may contact us about privacy questions or deletion of support correspondence.
