Privacy Policy (LumaChoice)

1. Overview

LumaChoice helps you make everyday choices. Its suggestions are for reference. You can use the app without registering or signing in.

2. On-device records

Option text, decision results, commitments, reflections and your Mind Traces history are processed on your device. Records you save stay on your device. LumaChoice does not send their contents to Aptabase or sync them to its servers.

3. Usage analytics

From version 1.08, LumaChoice uses Aptabase in the European Union to understand how the app is used. The app sends events such as opening the app, entering a feature or completing an action, together with the feature name and technical information such as app version, OS and system language. Aptabase derives country or region from the IP address without storing the address itself.

Aptabase privacy policy

4. Advertising and tracking

LumaChoice does not use analytics for advertising or cross-app tracking. Analytics events are not linked to an account or a persistent device identifier.

5. Retention and contact

Uninstalling the app removes its records from the device. Aptabase handles analytics retention under its own privacy policy. For questions about LumaChoice, email rtx3070757@gmail.com.
