LumaChoice · Privacy Policy

Read the complete privacy policy for this app.

Updated 24 November 2026 · Analytics applies from version 1.08

Full published policy

1. Purpose

LumaChoice helps you make everyday choices. Use the compass to pick from several options, flip a decision coin, or compare options to rank what matters to you. You can also make a commitment or work through guided questions.

2. Data privacy

You can use LumaChoice without an account. The core decision tools work on your device.

3. Usage analytics

From version 1.08, LumaChoice uses Aptabase in the European Union to understand which features people use and where they stop or repeat an action. The app sends usage events, such as app launches, feature visits and completed actions, with a fixed feature name. Aptabase also receives technical information such as app version, operating system version and system language. It uses the IP address to infer a country or region and does not store the address itself. We do not use this data for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Aptabase privacy policy

4. Records on your device

Options you enter, decision results, commitments, reflections and Mind Traces records stay on your device. The analytics events do not contain any of that content, account details or persistent device identifiers. Uninstalling the app removes its records from the device. Aptabase handles analytics retention under its own privacy policy.

5. Pact review notifications

When you seal and confirm a commitment, LumaChoice schedules a local notification for 10:00 a.m. on its review date. If notifications are allowed, the notification can display the commitment you entered. You can manage this in your device’s notification settings.

6. Using the results

LumaChoice is a decision aid. Its suggestions are for reference. You decide what to do.
