MerchPic · Privacy Policy

Product photos are prepared on your device. MerchPic does not upload them.

2026-10-23

Scope

This policy covers MerchPic for iPhone and iPad, provided by SmallToolWorks. No account is required. Imported photos, white-background images, and check results stay in the app on your device. MerchPic does not upload photos to us. The current app does not include advertising or usage-analytics software.

Permissions and your choices

The system photo picker provides only the photos you select. Files are read when you choose them in the system file picker. The camera is used only when you take a product photo. Saving prepared images to Photos, including a named album you can select in a seller app, asks for photo library permission. You can decline that permission and use Files or sharing instead. Manage permissions in iOS Settings.

Local storage

The app stores the product name, photo order, use labels, marketplace and category choices, imported image copies, the optional white-background image, and the check report in its own storage on the device. An imported copy is resized for use in the app. It is not a byte-for-byte copy of the original file. The app does not retouch or redraw the product. Deleting a product in the app removes that product’s files from the app. Photos and files already saved outside the app remain. Deleting the app removes its local data. Device backups are managed by your device.

Sharing and help links

If you share or save an export, the files go to the app, album, or service you choose, under that destination’s own terms. If you open a marketplace’s official requirements, that page opens in your browser. Preparing and checking photos does not send those photos over the network.

Support and updates

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send, and we use them to respond. Send only what is needed to explain the issue. Contact us to ask a privacy question or to request deletion of support correspondence. We update this policy when data practices change.
