PressPDFly · Privacy Policy

PDF compression runs on your device. PDF contents are not uploaded for processing. Limited analytics, Apple install attribution and purchases may use network connections.

2026-10-12

Scope and local files

PressPDFly compresses one PDF at a time. Imported documents, passwords, page content and compressed results are processed on your device and are not uploaded to us. Working files and preferences may be kept locally. Copies you export to Files, another app or a backup are managed separately.

File permissions

Access to a document selected through the system file picker is used to import and process that document. No account is required.

Limited product analytics

PressPDFly uses Aptabase in the EU region. It sends fixed feature and outcome categories and coarse ranges for imports, password unlocking, compression, exports, paywall use, purchases and restores. It does not send PDF content, file names or paths, passwords, exact file sizes or page counts, free-form input or raw errors.

Technical analytics data

The SDK may attach timestamps, temporary session identifiers, language, app/build and system versions, device model and Debug/Release status. Aptabase may process IP addresses and User-Agent to derive coarse region and a daily changing pseudonymous identifier. Its policy describes retention of up to five years; without a persistent personal identifier, individual events may be difficult to locate for deletion. The app has no dedicated analytics opt-out setting.

Apple install attribution

Apple AdServices may provide App Store advertising source categories such as campaign or keyword identifiers and country. Limited attribution categories are sent to product analytics. PDF content is not used for attribution.

Purchases

Apple handles single-export and lifetime purchases. The app reads transaction and entitlement information and retains local credit and transaction bookkeeping to unlock exports. We do not receive payment-card details or your Apple ID. Limited purchase and restore outcomes are included in analytics. The current app does not display third-party ads.

Retention and your choices

You can remove the app to remove its local app data. Exported copies and backups are managed separately. Core document processing is local; analytics, attribution and purchases may connect to the internet.

Support and changes

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send and use them to respond. Contact us for privacy questions or deletion of support correspondence. This policy will be updated when data practices change.
