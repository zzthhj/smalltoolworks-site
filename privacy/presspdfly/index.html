PressPDFly · Privacy Policy

PDF compression runs on your device. PDF contents are not uploaded for processing. Limited analytics, purchases and Google AdMob ads may use network connections.

2026-10-28

Scope and local files

PressPDFly compresses one PDF at a time. Imported documents, passwords, page content and compressed results are processed on your device and are not uploaded to us. Working files and preferences may be kept locally. Copies you export to Files, another app or a backup are managed separately.

File permissions

Access to a document selected through the system file picker is used to import and process that document. No account is required.

Limited product analytics

PressPDFly uses Aptabase in the EU region. It sends fixed categories and coarse ranges for app launches, imports, compression and export outcomes, and purchase outcomes. It does not send PDF content, file names or paths, passwords, exact file sizes or page counts, free-form input or raw errors.

Technical analytics data

The SDK may attach timestamps, temporary session identifiers, language, app/build and system versions, device model and Debug/Release status. Aptabase may process IP addresses and User-Agent to derive coarse region and a daily changing pseudonymous identifier. Its policy describes retention of up to five years; without a persistent personal identifier, individual events may be difficult to locate for deletion. The app has no dedicated analytics opt-out setting.

Advertising

PressPDFly is free to use and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob. To request, deliver and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect device information, device identifiers such as the advertising identifier when available, approximate location derived from the IP address, and ad interaction data. We do not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission, so the advertising identifier is not used to track you across apps or websites. Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland) we ask for consent through Google's User Messaging Platform; you can change or withdraw that consent at any time in the app under Settings → Ad privacy settings. Google processes ad data under its privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). PDF content, file names and compressed results are never shared with advertising partners.

Purchases

Apple handles single-export and lifetime purchases. A single-export purchase skips the ad for that export; a lifetime purchase removes ads permanently. The app reads transaction and entitlement information and keeps local credit and transaction bookkeeping to unlock exports. We do not receive payment-card details or your Apple ID. Limited purchase outcomes are included in analytics.

Retention and your choices

Document processing runs locally; analytics, purchases and ads may connect to the internet. Google retains ad data under its own policy.

Support and changes

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send and use them to respond. Contact us for privacy questions or deletion of support correspondence. This policy will be updated when data practices change.
