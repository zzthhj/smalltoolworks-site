1. Privacy Summary

SizeFixer (the “App”) prepares photos for upload: compressing file size, converting formats, and resizing dimensions. All photo processing happens on your device.

To improve the App and better serve our users, the App sends limited product-interaction analytics. No photos or image content ever leave your device.

2. Photos & Local Data

Photo-library and file access are used only to:

  • select photos from your library or import image files;
  • save processed results to Files or Photos when you choose.

All compression, conversion, resizing, and cropping is performed locally on your device. Your photos are never uploaded to our servers.

The face-redaction feature uses Apple’s Vision framework, entirely on your device, to locate faces so that redaction rectangles can be drawn over them. No face data or detection results are stored or transmitted.

3. Exported Files

When you export, SizeFixer may remove hidden metadata, such as GPS and camera EXIF, from output files. Only the image data you choose to save is written to your device.

4. Limited Analytics

We use Aptabase for privacy-focused analytics. We may record app launches, which features you use, how many items were imported, whether processing or export succeeded, paywall interactions, and purchase and restore outcomes.

We do not send photos, image content, file names, text recognized in images, barcode or QR-code contents, names, email addresses, device advertising identifiers (such as IDFA), or free-form text.

Aptabase may receive basic technical information supplied by its SDK, such as a temporary session ID, app and system version, device model, and language/region. Its service temporarily processes IP address and User-Agent to generate coarse location and a daily-changing pseudonymous identifier. Analytics is processed in Aptabase’s European Union region and may be retained for up to five years. See the Aptabase Privacy Policy.

If you install the App after tapping an App Store ad, Apple’s official attribution interface may return source details such as the ad campaign, keyword, and country or region. We record these anonymous numeric identifiers together with purchase events, solely to understand advertising performance; none of these statistics can be linked to you.

5. Advertising, Tracking, and Accounts

The App contains no advertising, does not share data with advertising networks, and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. No account or login is required.

6. In-App Purchases

Optional save credits and subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment card or Apple ID details. We receive only the purchase status needed to unlock features, plus limited analytics such as plan category and purchase outcome.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to rtx3070757@gmail.com.

Additional details

Purpose

SizeFixer provides practical photo editing and export utilities.

Photos and local processing

Compression, format conversion, resizing, cropping, rotation, borders, watermarking, redaction, metadata removal and background tools run on your device. Images are not uploaded to us for processing. Face detection for redaction uses Apple Vision locally; detection results are not stored or transmitted as biometric records. Metadata is read locally as needed to process or remove it and is not sent in analytics. Exported images remain in the destination you choose.

Permissions

File, photo-library or camera access is used for the features you choose, such as importing, capturing or saving content. You can manage camera and photo permissions in iOS Settings. No account or sign-in is required.

Limited product analytics

We use Aptabase in its European Union region to understand feature usage and improve the app. Events are sent automatically when analytics is active; they are not a report you must submit manually.

App launches, tools used, coarse imported-item counts and duration ranges, processing and export outcomes, paywall interactions, and purchase or restore outcomes. Image content, metadata, file names, paths, OCR text and barcode contents are not sent.

The SDK attaches technical fields such as timestamps, temporary session identifiers, app and build versions, SDK and operating-system versions, device model, language and Debug/Release status. Aptabase processes the request IP address and User-Agent to derive a coarse region and a daily changing pseudonymous identifier. We do not send your name, email address, Apple ID, photos, advertising identifier or a persistent device identifier to Aptabase.

According to Aptabase's published policy, analytics may be retained for up to five years. Without a persistent personal identifier, it may not be possible to locate an individual person's events for deletion. See the Aptabase privacy policy.

Apple Ads attribution

The app uses Apple's AdServices interface to measure installs from App Store ads. It may exchange an attribution token with Apple and receive campaign, ad-group, keyword and country or region information. Limited source identifiers and attribution results are included in Aptabase events to measure campaign performance. This does not upload your photos or use an IDFA.

Purchases and advertising

The current app does not display third-party advertisements. Optional purchases, credits or subscriptions are handled by Apple. The app reads transaction and entitlement information to unlock access; we do not receive your payment-card details or Apple ID. Limited plan categories and purchase outcomes may be recorded in product analytics.

Your choices and retention

You can manage permissions, remove saved content where the app provides deletion controls, and uninstall the app to remove its local app data. Copies saved to Photos, Files or device backups are managed separately. Core processing is local; analytics, attribution and App Store purchases may use network connections. This app does not currently offer a dedicated analytics opt-out switch.

Support and policy changes

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send, and use them to respond. Send only the information needed to explain your issue. You may contact us about privacy questions or deletion of support correspondence.
