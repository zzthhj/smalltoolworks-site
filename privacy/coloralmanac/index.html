ColorAlmanac · Privacy Policy

ColorAlmanac for iPhone and iPad is provided by SmallToolWorks and requires no account.

2026-11-28

Scope and local data

ColorAlmanac for iPhone and iPad is provided by SmallToolWorks and requires no account. Your birth date, the color method you choose, the time you are looking at and your language preference are stored on your device. Every color reading — the personal card, the continuous almanac, the month view and the color finder — is calculated on the device. These values are not uploaded to our servers and there is no cloud sync between devices.

Permissions

The camera is used only while the color finder is open; the system photo picker provides only the item you select. Notifications are requested only when you turn reminders on, and the app schedules them locally on the device rather than through a server. ColorAlmanac does not request location, microphone, contacts or photo-library write access. You can change permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

Trial, purchases and restoring

Apple processes purchases of ColorAlmanac Pro. The app verifies StoreKit transactions; we do not receive your card details or Apple Account password. Available plans are a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription and a one-time lifetime purchase; prices and any introductory offer are shown by the App Store. A three-day trial starts the first time the app opens, and its start is kept in the device Keychain so that reinstalling does not restart it. Restoring a purchase uses the same Apple Account. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription; subscriptions are managed in App Store settings.

Anonymous usage counts

ColorAlmanac sends a limited set of anonymous counts to Aptabase in the EU region. The events are app starts, completing personal setup, calendar views (list or month), opening the finder, a finder result or a blocked finder with a reason code, reminder outcomes, paywall views, a failed product catalogue request, and purchase or restore outcomes with the plan name. Properties contain fixed categories only. Your birth date, sun sign, birthstone, birth month, the colors produced, photos you frame and anything you type are never included. System information includes app and build version, OS version, device model, language and timestamps. There are no advertising SDKs, no advertising attribution and no cross-app or cross-site tracking.

Analytics processing and control

Aptabase temporarily processes the connection IP address and User-Agent to derive country or region and a daily per-app pseudonymous identifier. It states that IP addresses and User-Agent are not stored and that salts rotate daily, and it retains analytics for up to five years. This approximate location is derived from the connection, not from the device location services. The counts are anonymous and start with the app; there is no separate analytics switch inside ColorAlmanac. Because the counts are not linked to your identity, an individual event may not be identifiable for a deletion request.

Retention and deletion

Preferences stay on your device until you change them or remove the app. Removing ColorAlmanac removes its local data, including the stored profile and the trial record on that device. iOS device backups and any copies you make are managed separately by you. Purchases remain tied to your Apple Account.
