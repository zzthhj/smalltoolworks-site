StillTap · Privacy Policy

Read the complete privacy policy for this app.

Migrated to this website: 11 October 2026

Full published policy

1. Purpose: StillTap is a lightweight calming and mindfulness tool. It provides simple practices such as wooden fish tapping, prayer beads, incense timer, singing bowl sounds, and breathing guidance.

2. Data Privacy: StillTap does not collect, store, sell, or share your personal data. The app is designed to work fully offline — the only network requests it makes are limited anonymous usage statistics, described in Section 3.

3. Anonymous Analytics: To improve the product, StillTap records anonymous feature-usage and purchase-funnel events via the third-party service Aptabase — for example, which practice feature was used, the duration range, and whether a paywall was opened or a purchase completed. These events contain fixed choices and time buckets only, and never include practice content, personal identity, or device identifiers. If you install the app after tapping an App Store ad, Apple’s official attribution API returns source details such as the ad keyword and country, used solely to measure ad effectiveness. None of these statistics can be linked to you personally. You can disable analytics reporting by restricting network access for the app in system settings.

4. Local Practice Data: Practice records, such as tap counts, bead counts, meditation minutes, streaks, preferences, sound settings, and Pro status, are stored locally on your device.

  • Your practice data is not uploaded to any server.
  • No account is required.
  • We do not track your personal activity outside the App.

5. Sound, Haptics & Notifications: StillTap may use local sound playback, haptic feedback, and optional reminders.

  • Sound and haptic feedback are processed locally on your device.
  • If reminders are enabled, they are used only for local notifications.
  • You can manage notification permissions in your device settings.

6. No Medical Purpose: StillTap is not a medical app. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or therapy. The App is intended only for relaxation, focus, and personal mindfulness practice.

7. Support: rtx3070757@gmail.com
