PixDim · Privacy Policy

Images are processed on your device. Limited usage analytics use Aptabase in the EU; there are no third-party ads.

2026-10-18

Scope and image processing

This policy covers PixDim for iPhone and iPad, provided by SmallToolWorks. No account is required. Imported images and processed results are handled on your device, not uploaded to us. PixDim does not overwrite originals. Exported images omit original EXIF location metadata.

Permissions and your choices

The system photo picker provides only the images you select. Files are accessed when you choose them through the system file picker. Saving to Photos requests add-only access. You can decline photo-saving permission and use Files or sharing instead. Manage permissions in iOS Settings.

Local storage and retention

Recent settings, saved rules, verified transaction records, unused export credits and fingerprints of unlocked results are stored locally. Saved rules contain processing parameters. Temporary export files are normally removed after saving, sharing or cancellation. You can clear saved rules in Settings; deleting the app removes its local app data. Exported copies and device backups are managed separately.

Payments and restoration

Apple handles payments and processes purchase information under its own policies. PixDim uses verified StoreKit transactions and entitlement information to unlock exports. We do not receive your payment-card details or Apple Account password. Single exports are consumable; unused credits are local and used exports cannot be restored. Lifetime access can be restored with the purchasing Apple Account.

Usage analytics in the EU

PixDim sends fixed event categories for startup, task selection, imports, processing, result previews, comparison use, export attempts and outcomes, paywall views, purchase outcomes and restoration to Aptabase in the EU region. Properties contain only fixed operation, source, destination, purchase-plan, outcome and failure categories. No images, filenames, paths, image settings, exact dimensions or file sizes, transaction identifiers, free-form input or raw error messages are sent. The SDK also sends timestamps, temporary session identifiers, language, app/build and operating-system versions, device model and Debug/Release status. Aptabase processes IP addresses and User-Agent to derive coarse country/region and a pseudonymous identifier that changes daily for each app. Its policy states retention of up to five years; without persistent identifiers, individual analytics records may not be identifiable for deletion. There is no dedicated analytics opt-out switch in the app. Processing images remains local; analytics and purchases may use network connections.

Advertising and sharing

PixDim has no advertising SDK or advertising attribution integration. We do not operate a server that receives your images. Exported copies in other apps, cloud-backed Files locations, Photos and backups are handled under those services’ settings and policies.

Support, requests and updates

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send, and use them to respond. Contact us for privacy questions or deletion of support correspondence. Send only what is needed to explain the issue. We update this policy when data practices change.
