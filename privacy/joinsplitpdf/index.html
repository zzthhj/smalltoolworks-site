JoinSplit PDF · Privacy Policy

2026-10-14

Local documents

JoinSplit PDF merges, splits and organizes PDFs on your device. Imported PDF content, file names, pages and editing results are processed locally and are not uploaded to us. No account registration is required.

Saved task copies

When you choose to save progress, the app stores PDF content, file names and editing state locally for up to five tasks. You can delete these copies from Home. Saved task copies do not have an opening password. Export passwords and undo history are not included.

File access and passwords

The system file picker provides access to documents you select. Passwords used for unlocking or exporting are kept in the current task and are not saved with task copies. Exported files and copies stored in Files, other apps or backups are managed separately by you.

Purchases

Apple processes single-credit and lifetime purchases. The app reads transaction and entitlement information and keeps a local credit and transaction ledger to unlock exports. We do not receive your payment card information or Apple ID. Purchases and restoration may connect to Apple services.

Analytics and advertising

We use Aptabase in the EU region to understand app usage and improve reliability. Events cover app opening, tool selection, import results, purchase options, purchase/restoration results and completed or cancelled exports. Custom properties use fixed categories and coarse quantity ranges. The SDK also sends a temporary session ID, timestamps, device model, app/system version and language. The service processes IP address and User-Agent to derive country/region and a daily rotating anonymous hash; the IP address itself is not stored. We do not send PDF contents, file names or paths, passwords, transaction IDs or Apple ID. We do not use advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking. Automated tests do not send events; Debug and Release events are separated. Apple purchase and rating services may also use the network.

Your choices

Delete saved task copies in the app, or uninstall the app to remove its local app data. Manage exported copies and device backups separately. Access to files, device protection and backups is controlled by your device settings and the services you choose.

Support and updates

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send, and use them to respond. Contact us for privacy questions or requests about support emails. This policy will be updated when data handling changes.
