ScanVela · Privacy Policy

Everything happens on your device. ScanVela does not upload your documents and does not track you.

2026-11-18

Scope and local processing

This policy covers ScanVela for iPhone, provided by SmallToolWorks. No account is required. Photos, scanned pages, imported PDFs and exported files are processed and stored on your device only. ScanVela does not upload your documents, and we do not receive copies of them.

Permissions and your choices

Camera access is requested only when you scan with the camera, and captured images stay on your device. Importing from Photos uses the system photo picker, which provides only the images you select. Importing from Files uses the system file picker. You can manage permissions in iOS Settings at any time.

Local storage and retention

Documents you keep are stored in the app's local storage, which you can also browse in the Files app. Temporary export files are normally removed after saving, sharing or cancellation. Deleting the app removes its local app data. There is no server-side copy of your documents.

Payments and restoration

Apple handles payments and processes purchase information under its own policies. ScanVela uses verified StoreKit transactions to unlock Pro features: 300 DPI print-grade exports and password-protected PDFs. A single Pro export is consumable and is used once; unused export credits are stored locally in the device Keychain and remain available after reinstalling the app. Lifetime access can be restored with the purchasing Apple Account. We do not receive your payment-card details or Apple Account password.

No analytics, no tracking

ScanVela contains no analytics SDK, no advertising, no attribution and no third-party tracking code, and it sends no usage data to us. Nothing about you or your documents is collected. App Store purchases are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Support, requests and updates

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send, and use them to respond. Contact us for privacy questions or deletion of support correspondence. Send only what is needed to explain the issue. We update this policy when data practices change.
