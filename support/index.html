Before you write

Answers to the usual questions

How do I request a refund?

Purchases made through the App Store are handled by Apple. The fastest route is reportaproblem.apple.com, which lists your recent purchases and lets you request a refund directly.

If you bought through a different route, email us with the App Store order identifier and we will take it from there.

A feature says Pro but I have not subscribed. Why?

Purchases can take a moment to sync after the App Store confirms them. Force-quit the app and reopen it. If the Pro badge is still showing after that, email us with your App Store ID.

Note that in SeeClear, core red-green and blue-yellow color naming is free and does not require Pro.

OmniPDF shows an ad while exporting. Can I remove it?

No. OmniPDF is free to use and a short ad may be shown when you export a processed document. That is the whole model — there is no paid tier that removes it.

If the ad itself fails to load, blocks your export, or behaves incorrectly, that is a bug. Please report it.

Are my photos or documents uploaded to your servers?

Wherever a task allows it, processing happens on your device. Not every operation can be local, and where something is transmitted it is stated in that app's privacy policy.

See each app's policy for the specifics: OmniPDF, SeeClear, SizeFixer, NativeID.

NativeID rejected my photo. Can you fix it?

NativeID prepares photos to the size and layout requirements it holds for a given country and document type. We cannot see the decision made by an issuing authority, and requirements change.

Check the country and document type carefully, then use the framing checks in the app. If the specification held in the app looks wrong or out of date, tell us which country and document, and we will review it.

How do I report a privacy concern?

Email us with the app name and a description of what happened. We treat privacy reports as priority mail and will respond to them ahead of routine support.

Our policies are listed on the privacy index.

Do you offer refunds outside the App Store?

Where a purchase is made directly from us, we will review refund requests on a case-by-case basis. Include the purchase receipt and the reason for the request.
