About

A small studio building small tools

SmallToolWorks is an independent software studio focused on practical iPhone and iPad apps. We build small, focused tools for specific everyday tasks, with an emphasis on simplicity, privacy and useful results.

Every app we ship starts the same way: a specific annoyance that keeps costing people time. A PDF that will not print because six pages are blank. A photo that is too large to send. An ID photo that is the wrong size, again. A color you cannot quite tell apart.

We would rather solve one of those properly than solve fifty of them shallowly. Each app has a reason to exist on its own, and we keep adding tools when we find a task worth solving.

What we value

Clear scope

If a feature does not help the core task, it does not ship. We would rather you find the app obvious than impressive.

Privacy by default

Wherever an app can complete a task on your device, it does. Where something must be transmitted, we say so in that app's privacy policy rather than burying it. Each app has its own policy, because the answers genuinely differ.

Straight pricing

Free means free, and paid features are marked as paid. In OmniPDF, every tool is free and a short ad may appear on export — that is the entire model, with nothing hidden behind a second tier.

No dark patterns

No artificial countdowns, no nagging for reviews in the middle of a task, no pre-checked add-ons. We would rather earn the install than pressure people into it.

How we work

Small team, direct contact. If something breaks, you write to an address that reaches the people who wrote the code. Feedback on what is confusing or missing genuinely changes what gets built next.

Honest limitations

Some things are outside what our apps do. NativeID prepares photos to size and layout requirements — it does not create, issue or modify identity documents. SeeClear assists with everyday color perception; it is not a medical device and should not be used for safety-critical decisions. We would rather state a boundary plainly than let an assumption build.
