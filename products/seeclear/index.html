SeeClear

Everyday color assistance for color vision deficiency.

See colors with more confidence. SeeClear is a practical color assistant designed for people with color vision deficiency — it helps you name, compare and check colors in everyday situations.

Safety notice SeeClear is designed for everyday visual assistance only. It is not a medical diagnostic tool and should not be used for traffic, safety-critical, or medical decisions. Color identification results are an aid, not an authority — verify anything that matters.
What it does

Built around the questions you actually ask

Most color problems in daily life are comparisons: this against that, will these go together, is this the shade I think it is.

Identify colors

Pick a point on screen or a region of a photo and get a readable color name and value.

Compare colors

Place two colors side by side and see how close they really are, including a measured difference.

Color enhancement

Adjust the image so differences that were hard to see become easier to distinguish.

Outfit color analysis

Check how garments work together before committing to a combination.

Photo comparison

Compare two photos of the same or similar items to see how a color reads differently.

Contrast & color checks

Check contrast between colors and review multi-color combinations.

Color library

Keep a personal reference of colors you care about, so you can match against them later.

Find color

Search your saved library for the closest match to a color you can see.

The core stays free

Always free

Color naming for red-green and yellow-blue distinctions stays free. It is the core of the app and it is not placed behind a paywall or a trial timer.

  • Identify red, green, yellow and blue tones
  • Basic color naming in photos and on screen

Pro tools

More advanced workflows — fine-grained color comparison, chart recognition, photo verification and the color library — are available through Pro features. Current pricing is listed in the App Store.

  • Precision color comparison
  • Chart and multi-color recognition
  • Contrast checking and photo verification
  • Full color library

On-device by default

Color analysis runs on your device. Your photos are not uploaded to be analyzed.

No account needed

SeeClear works without sign-in. Nothing here requires a profile or a password.
