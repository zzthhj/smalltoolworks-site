JobPacket: Quotes & Invoices

The quote, the site photos, the work record, the invoice and every payment receipt for one job — kept together on your iPhone or iPad.

Get JobPacket on the App Store Get support Privacy Policy

Made for one person on site

JobPacket is built for independent tradespeople who work alone: plumbers, electricians, installers, handymen and small contractors. You write the quote, do the work, invoice it and take the deposit without leaving the customer's driveway.

Price the job before you start

  • Write down what the customer needs and capture site photos while you are standing there.
  • Price the work with itemized quantities and rates instead of one guessed figure.
  • Create a quote PDF and choose which site photos go into it.
  • When the customer agrees, carry on with the same job rather than retyping it into a second app.

Document what you actually did

  • Organize photos by Before, During, Issues and After.
  • Record what you did, what you found and what still needs attention.
  • Type the notes or use on-device dictation where supported.
  • Keep materials and job expenses next to the work record, not in a separate note.

Invoice, deposits and the balance

See the agreed price, invoice totals, money received and the remaining balance in one place. Record deposits, part payments and the final payment, and produce a separate PDF receipt for each one. Share the paid invoice once the job is settled. If an invoice would take the total past the agreed quote, JobPacket asks you to confirm the additional charge with the customer first.

A report the customer can keep

Build a PDF with the work performed, the results, the photos you choose and the customer's acknowledgment. Preview it before you send it. Finalized reports keep their saved versions, so the copy the customer reviewed stays available even when you change the job afterwards — a later change becomes a new version. Acknowledgment can be a signature, or it can be recorded as customer not present, declined or not requested, and the PDF says which.

Find the last visit

Search by customer, site or job to review earlier quotes, photos and service records. Export a backup, or hand work over between devices with a job file.

On your device

Job records, photos, signatures and PDFs stay on your iPhone or iPad. No account, no cloud photo storage, no advertising. Optional dictation and writing help use Apple models on the device. Limited usage analytics (Aptabase, EU region) are described in the Privacy Policy. Recording work, completing jobs, previewing documents, recording payments and sharing individual payment receipts are free. Pro unlocks exporting, sharing and printing quote, work report and invoice PDFs. Previously exported PDF versions remain available after Pro expires. Weekly, monthly and yearly subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase are available; monthly and yearly plans offer a 7-day free trial to eligible customers. Prices and eligibility are shown in the App Store.

The launch listing is on the United States App Store. The App Store shows what your Apple Account region can install.
