JobPacket

Record a field service job on site — photos, work done, parts, customer sign-off — and hand over a finished PDF before you leave.

Get support Privacy Policy

Finish the job before you drive away

  • Start with just a job title, or skip the details and fill them in at the door.
  • Capture Before, Progress, Issue and After photos with the continuous camera or from the photo library.
  • Add work notes by typing or on-device dictation, plus materials and issues, while you are still on site.
  • Set the outcome, finalize, and share the PDF on the spot. The record for this visit is already written.

Keep the record for the next visit

Every finished job stays on the device with its customer, service address, photos, work performed, parts and recommendations. Next time you can search a customer, a site or a job and start from what happened last visit instead of a blank form.

The version you hand over is frozen

Finalizing freezes a packet version: the client-facing PDF keeps what the customer actually reviewed, even if you edit the job later. A revision becomes a new version and the earlier one stays available. Client acknowledgment can be a signature, or recorded as client not present, declined, or not requested — the PDF says which.

On your device

Job records, photos, signatures and PDFs stay on your iPhone or iPad. No account, no cloud photo storage, no advertising. Optional dictation and writing help use Apple models on the device. Limited usage analytics (Aptabase, EU region) are described in the Privacy Policy. The first two finalized Job Packets are free; Pro unlocks unlimited jobs with a subscription or a lifetime purchase handled by the App Store.
