MerchPic

Import real product photos, check them on your device, and export JPEG sets for marketplace listings.

Get support Privacy Policy

One product, one prepared set

  • Import up to 24 photos of one product from Photos, the camera, or Files, and keep the order you choose.
  • Mark how each photo is used, choose the main image, and reorder the set.
  • Make an optional white-background main image on the device. The imported photo stays available.
  • Export a JPEG set for each marketplace you select, then share it or save it to Photos. Originals outside the app are not overwritten.

Marketplace presets

Amazon, Shopify, Etsy, eBay, and TikTok Shop each use their own size, count, file-size, and background preset. A general preset is included. The app also checks sharpness, text, size, similar photos, and color, and separates problems that block export from ones you need to confirm yourself.

Confirm before you upload. Rules vary by site, category, and seller account. A preset is not a promise that the listing will pass review.

On your device

Photos are prepared on your iPhone or iPad. The current app has no account, no ads, and no in-app purchase. It does not upload your photos.
