NativeID

ID and passport photo preparation.

Prepare ID and passport photos with the right size and layout. Select your country and the document you need, capture or import a photo, and export a correctly sized, correctly framed result with the print layout already arranged.

Get NativeID on the App Store Get support
iPhone & iPad Multiple countries Print layouts included
What NativeID does and does not do NativeID only helps prepare photos to specified size and layout requirements. It does not create, issue, reproduce or modify official identity documents. It is a photo preparation tool, not a document service.
How it works

Seven steps, no guesswork

Requirements differ by country and by document. NativeID keeps them separate so you always work to the right specification.

1. Select country

Choose the country whose requirements you need to follow.

2. Select photo type

Pick the specific document and photo variant, each with its own dimensions.

3. Capture or import

Take a photo with guided framing, or import one you already have.

4. Adjust background

Adjust the background to meet the requirements for that document.

5. Check framing

Verify head position, framing and eye-line placement against the specification.

6. Export photo

Export at the exact pixel dimensions and format the document requires.

7. Create print layout

Arrange multiple copies on a printable sheet for photo studios and offices.

Features

What else it handles

Country-specific templates

Size, aspect ratio and framing rules are stored per country and per document type, so you are never applying a rule from the wrong place.

Passport photo sizing

Export at the exact dimensions required for passport applications, with the head-to-frame proportions applied for you.

Background adjustment

Replace or even out a background to meet plain or off-white requirements where they apply.

Face positioning

Guided capture and framing checks help place the face where the specification expects it.

Print layouts

Generate a printable sheet with multiple copies, so you can hand over a printed page instead of a file to send around.

Photo history

Keep previously prepared photos so you can re-export or revise without starting over.

Processed on device

Photo adjustment and export happen on your device wherever the task allows it.

No identity data

The app handles a photo and its dimensions. It does not create, store or issue identity records or documents.

Local history

Photo history is kept on your device so you stay in control of your images.
