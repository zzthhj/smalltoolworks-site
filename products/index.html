SeeClear

An everyday color assistant designed for people with color vision deficiency. Identify colors, compare them, and check how garments work together before you commit.

  • Color recognition and naming
  • Red-green and blue-yellow assistance
  • Color enhancement for difficult differences
  • Photo comparison
  • Outfit color analysis
  • Color library and contrast checks
iPhone iPad On-device processing
Learn more App Store
Not a medical device SeeClear is for everyday assistance only. It is not intended for medical diagnosis, traffic-signal decisions or any safety-critical use.
