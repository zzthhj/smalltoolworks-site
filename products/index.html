Photos

PixDim

Resize, compress and convert images on your device.

  • Set pixel dimensions, keep the aspect ratio or choose Fit, Fill or Stretch for an exact canvas.
  • Set a target file size or adjust encoding quality. Export as JPEG, PNG or HEIC when supported by your device.
  • Compare the original and result, including actual dimensions, format and file size, before paying.

Choose a single export to unlock one processed result, or lifetime access for unlimited single-image exports. The same unlocked result can be saved or shared again on this device without a second payment. No subscription. Current local prices are shown in the app.

MerchPic

Prepare real product photos for marketplace listings on your device.

  • Import up to 24 photos of one product and keep your order.
  • Choose the main image and, if you want, make a white-background version on the device.
  • Export a JPEG set for each selected marketplace. Originals outside the app stay untouched.

These are preparation presets for Amazon, Shopify, Etsy, eBay, TikTok Shop, and a general set. They do not mean a marketplace has approved the listing.
