ScanVela

Turn photos, scans and long screenshots into clean, ready-to-share PDFs — arranged, flattened and compressed on your device.

Get support Privacy Policy

From messy pages to one tidy PDF

  • Scan with the camera — automatic edge detection finds the page and crops it straight.
  • Import photos or PDF files, then drag pages into the order you want.
  • Arrange one, two or four images per page — ideal for IDs, certificates and photo sets.
  • Flatten curved book pages with an on-device machine learning model.

Export the way you need it

  • Export as PDF, as one image per page, or as a single long stitched image.
  • Set a target PDF size and preview your output. The app tells you if it cannot reach the target.
  • Keep everything in the Files app or share it directly. Originals are not overwritten.

On your device

Scanning, arranging and exporting at 200 DPI are free, with no watermark — PDF export never costs per page. Pro adds 300 DPI print-grade exports and password-protected PDFs, unlocked with a single Pro export or a one-time lifetime purchase — no subscription. Prices are shown in the app.

Documents are processed on your device. ScanVela needs no account and shows no ads.
