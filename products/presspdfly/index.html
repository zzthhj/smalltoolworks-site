PressPDFly

Compress one PDF to a target size, preview the result, then save or share.

Get PressPDFly on the App StoreGet support

Designed around one PDF

  • Choose a target size: 500 KB, 1 MB, 2 MB, 5 MB or a custom limit.
  • Smart mode prioritizes text and document structure, compressing suitable image pages.
  • Scanned mode adjusts scanned images and skips pages with text layers or interactive content.
  • Extreme mode converts pages to images to try for a smaller file.
  • Compare file size and savings, preview the PDF, then adjust settings or export.

Purchases

Processing and preview are available in the app. Export uses a single-export purchase or a lifetime unlock. There is no subscription; available products and prices are shown in the app.

Check the result Compression depends on the source file and may not reach the target. Extreme mode may reduce clarity and remove selectable text, forms and annotations. Check the result before sharing.

Local processing

PDF compression runs on your device. PDF contents are not uploaded for processing. Limited analytics, Apple install attribution and purchases may use network connections.
