OmniPDF

One batch of files. Multiple steps. One workflow.

Process a batch of PDFs with shared settings. Combine compression, redaction, watermarks, passwords and page organization into a workflow, preview the results, then export when you are ready.

Get OmniPDF on the App StoreGet support

Batch processing

Set up the job once for multiple files.

  • Compress PDFs to a target file size.
  • Find and redact searchable sensitive text across PDFs.
  • Apply consistent watermarks and page numbering.
  • Set, change or remove opening passwords.
  • Correct orientation, remove blank pages, trim margins and adjust paper size.
  • Remove metadata and annotations, or flatten editable content.

Reusable workflows

Choose tools, set their execution order and save the sequence for later.

  • Combine compression, redaction and watermarks in one workflow.
  • Reuse saved workflows for recurring document tasks.
  • Preview the result before continuing or exporting.

A complete PDF toolkit

Handle individual documents as well as batches.

  • Merge, split, delete, extract, duplicate and reorder pages.
  • Rotate, crop, insert and replace pages.
  • Scan documents, convert photos to PDF and PDF pages to images.
  • Add text, annotations, signatures, stamps and dates; fill existing forms.
  • Manually redact selected areas of a page.

Optional PDF inspection

Inspect first if needed, or choose a tool and start directly.

  • Check file size, orientation, blank pages and password protection.
  • Review annotations, form fields, metadata and searchability.
  • Choose the operations that fit the inspection results.

Preview first. Unlock export when you need it.

Processing and result previews are available in the app. Exporting or sharing processed files requires an in-app purchase. Choose export credits or a monthly, yearly or lifetime plan; current options and prices are shown in the app and the App Store.

Local document processing

PDF processing runs on your device. Documents, OCR text, passwords and signatures are not uploaded to us. Recent-file copies and records are stored locally; limited product analytics are described in the privacy policy.
