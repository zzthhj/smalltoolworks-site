ColorAlmanac

One color card a day, with its code and the reasoning behind it. When you are choosing a shirt, an accessory, a desktop or a planner page, start from today's color.

Get support Privacy Policy

Choose the method you like

Five methods to pick from: Five Elements, sun sign, birthstone, Thai weekday colors and the double-hour color. Open the card and you get the recommended color, its code and why it was chosen.

The card is built like a printed swatch book: paper-white ground, ink headings, exact sRGB codes, and one large swatch over a fine line pattern.

Flip through the calendar and look ahead

Look up any date and time, run through the next few days, or see a whole month of colors at a glance. Tap a day and its card opens.

Read the colors around you

Point the camera at something, or pick a patch of a photo, to see its color family and code, then compare it with your own method and today's color.

A reminder at the time you choose

Before you leave in the morning, at lunch, or whenever it suits you: let today's color check in.

On your device

Color reading and color recognition both run on the device, with no account to create. Colors come from the reading rules and folk tradition, as a light reference for everyday styling.

The first launch gives you 72 hours of the full experience, and the trial does not charge you when it ends. To keep seeing colors and use the full feature set, choose a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription or a one-time purchase in the app.
