ColorAlmanac

One color at a time, chosen by the tradition you actually follow — worked out on your iPhone or iPad, with no account and no network.

Get support Privacy Policy

One method, one color

Pick the reading that matches what you follow. Only one method is active at a time, and the home card, the almanac, the finder and the reminders all read from it.

  • Five Elements from your birth date, the default: the stem of your birth day sets your personal element, and the day you are looking at ranks the five color families into preferred, secondary, usable and sparing.
  • Sun sign — worked out from a full birthday, or picked by hand if you have not entered one.
  • Birthstone — needs only a birth month.
  • Thai weekday colors — needs nothing at all: Sunday red, Monday yellow, Tuesday pink, Wednesday green, Thursday orange, Friday blue, Saturday purple.
  • The double-hour color — changes every two hours of the local day and names the trigram that shares its element.

Look at a moment before it arrives

Tap the time on the home card to jump three hours ahead, to 8am tomorrow, or to any date and clock time you choose. A future card names the target date and the local time it reads, and Back to now returns to live time.

Compare days, or read the month

Set a start date, a local clock time and a span of two to seven days. Switch to the month view to see one swatch per day at the same clock time, with the previous and next month a tap away. Open any day for its sample and the full reasoning behind it.

Read the color in front of you

Point the camera or choose a photo, and the finder reports which tier of your own method that color belongs to. It gives one result for your personal method: no generic color, no reference swatches and no conflict hints. The tier swatches are explanation only, and the finder never opens the camera by itself.

A paper swatch book, not a dashboard

The interface comes from printed color-sample books: a paper-white ground, ink headings, hairline dividers, and one large area of exact sRGB color with a fine line pattern. Body text follows your system text size, and dates and color values use tabular figures.

On your device

Every calculation happens on the iPhone or iPad. No account, no sign-in, no location and no network connection. Camera access runs only while the finder is open, and photos arrive through the system picker, so the app receives only the item you choose. Reminders are local notifications that you switch on yourself. Optional anonymous usage counts (Aptabase, EU region) and App Store purchases are described in the Privacy Policy.

Editing your profile is always free. The continuous almanac, the month view and the color finder need a valid trial or Pro. A three-day trial starts the first time you open the app; Pro is a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app.

The readings are for everyday color choices and cultural interest, not medical, financial or other advice. Not on the App Store yet — this page and the Privacy Policy are ready ahead of release.
